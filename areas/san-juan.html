Good to Know

Questions from San Juan

Do you serve San Juan?

Yes, and we are close. Our lab is in Pharr at 1002 W Sam Houston Blvd, Suite 9, inside the Pharr Medical Complex — San Juan sits directly alongside Pharr on the same corridor.

Do I need a doctor's order if I am coming from San Juan?

No. You can order your own testing yourself. No doctor's order and no referral are needed. If a San Juan provider gave you an order, bring it and we will follow it exactly.

Can you come to my house in San Juan?

Yes, and being this close makes it straightforward. Call us at (956) 586-6463 to ask about setting up a Home Draw.

How do I get to you from the Basilica?

Head west on the Business 83 or Expressway 83 corridor into Pharr and come off toward West Sam Houston Boulevard. We are inside the Pharr Medical Complex, Suite 9.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during our hours — Monday through Friday 7 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 11:30 AM.

Can I come back for repeat testing?

Yes, and being nearby makes that easy. If you are tracking something over time, call and we will tell you what to ask for on the next visit.
